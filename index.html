Our Story

Rooted in
Heritage

In the ancient highlands of Ethiopia — the birthplace of coffee — the Kafa region has cultivated the world's finest Arabica beans for over a millennium. KAFA NOIR was born from a deep reverence for this living legacy.

Every lot is hand-selected from small highland farms at elevations above 1,800 meters, where the volcanic soil, misty altitude, and equatorial sun create conditions no other region on earth can replicate.

We partner directly with generational farming families, honouring their craft through fair trade, transparent supply chains, and artisan roasting that amplifies — never masks — the natural brilliance of each harvest.

Process

Made with
Precision

04
01
Highland Selection

Each harvest is personally curated by our sourcing team at farms above 1,800m. Only cherries at peak Brix ripeness are accepted — a process that takes 3 to 4 weeks per season.

02
Natural Processing

Cherries are sun-dried on raised African beds for 21–28 days, allowing natural fermentation to develop the complex fruit and floral notes KAFA NOIR is celebrated for.

03
Artisan Roasting

Our master roasters craft each profile in small batches of 30kg, monitoring temperature curves in real-time on vintage drum roasters — blending science with instinct honed over decades.

04
Premium Packaging

Roasted and nitrogen-flushed within 24 hours. Sealed in matte black valve bags and dispatched via refrigerated export to maintain peak freshness at delivery.
